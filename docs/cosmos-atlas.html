What is settled and what is not

Reading this page honestly

Built bridges are in Rushi now. Most are still WORKING, awaiting your acceptance, and several of their citations are unopened. Source bridges name a text Baracus expects to support them; none has been opened yet, and some sources (the Yijing’s Xici, the Zuozhuan, the Liji, the Shizi) still need registering. Proposed bridges are Rushi’s own readings and will be drawn in crimson.

The Fermi Paradox is a modern question, so most of its links to these characters can only be interpretation. That is fine as long as each one says so.

Which attractor goes with which system is a design choice, like the placement of a star. The five attractors not yet in Rushi (Arneodo, Rabinovich–Fabrikant, Newton–Leipnik, Lorenz-84, Burke–Shaw) are drawn here with the parameters in common use. Their descriptions and citations are Baracus’s, from general knowledge, and unchecked.

Membership of IX–XIII is a draft for you to change. Many of these characters share a reading with ones already in Rushi (名 / 明, 一 / 意 / 義 / 易, 心 / 信), so their ids will need a gloss word.
